## C_Boot.kicad_sch

PS clock, QSPI boot and host supervision

## C_Boot.kicad_sch

25 MHz single-ended PS clock. Separate boot flash from STM32H745. Both MIO banks at 3.3 V. QSPI straps sampled at PS_POR_B release.

## C_FX20.kicad_sch

FX20 | parallel interface, USB PHY and supplies

## C_FX20.kicad_sch

## C_USB_C.kicad_sch

USB-C | two SuperSpeed lanes, USB 2.0 and attach detection

## C_USB_C.kicad_sch

Self-powered upstream port. Two Rd terminations for orientation, AC coupling on TX only, no external SuperSpeed lane switch.

## C_USB_C.kicad_sch

Review crystal load against selected crystal and stray capacitance. Verify connector loss, ESD package capacitance, ground strategy and Gen2x2 compliance.
