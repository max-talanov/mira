MIRA Rev.A — Power Monitor connection review
2026-10-05

Scope and validation

Reviewed POWER_MONITOR and its seven active child sheets, 3400 through 4000.
Connections were reconstructed from the native embedded symbol definitions,
rotation/mirror-aware pin geometry, wires, junctions and scoped labels. The
monitor signals were traced through the previously reviewed rail, programming
and Power Control sheets. Manufacturer pinouts and functions were checked.

An independent final comparison verified 242 symbol identities and all pin
positions and net partitions. Wires, labels, junctions and no-connect markers
are unchanged. The sole component change is R3609, described below. Parent and
child UUIDs, hierarchy paths, filenames and MIRA_RevA definitions are preserved.

The overall sheet contains an E4-style logical interconnection overview and
individual descriptions of all seven blocks. The graphical collector denotes
16 separate signal pairs; it is not an electrical short or a KiCad bus. Actual
connections remain the global nets in the native leaves. The leaf guides use
the same five headings. Blue notes distinguish repeated circuits. All eight
previews were visually checked and all new text fits the existing drawing
frames. Previews are approximate renders of native geometry, not KiCad plots.

The empty, unreferenced 3600_MONITOR_INPUT_AON_USB_SYS attachment shares the
3500 root UUID. It was left unchanged and excluded from the active bundle.

Native KiCad ERC, simulation, firmware/PCB validation and bench measurements
were not performed. This review confirms the stated schematic relationships,
not validated whole-system protection or shutdown timing.

Confirmed defect and completed correction

3600: R3609 = 10 kOhm and R3610 = 10 kOhm demanded MON_V_P15 = 7.5 V
at a 15 V rail, continuously loading the AON clamp and corrupting telemetry.
R3609 is now 47 kOhm. MON_V_P15 = P15 / 5.7, giving 2.6316 V at 15 V.
The native library ID, value, MPN and description now consistently identify
Panasonic ERJ1GNF4702C; the existing 0201 footprint and pin locations remain.
This scale also gives a nominal PG_P15 threshold of 13.4504 V at AON = 3.28 V.

Verified current sensing and nominal thresholds

The positive-rail INA301A1 channels have gain 20 V/V. Their 10 kOhm LIMIT
resistors set a nominal 0.800 V threshold from the nominal 80 uA source.
Grounded RESET selects transparent alerts. Current filters follow OUT and
do not delay the internal overcurrent comparator. Nominal values below omit
amplifier, limit-source, shunt, resistor and temperature errors.

U3501 INA293 supplies input-current telemetry. Its IN+ is BUS24_PRE_SHUNT
and IN- is BUS24. The current 400_INPUT_FILTER_SENSE leaf was not supplied.
The stated 20 mOhm input shunt is corroborated by the historical input-filter
reference (R203 between those same nets), but its current reference/value and
the PG_INPUT producer still require the actual current input subsystem.
All fifteen other shunt polarities and net names match the supplied rail sheets.

3400: U3401 OPA2991 uses AON for V+ and N15_RAW for V-. Channel A gives
20 x (N15 - N15_RAW), a positive return-current signal. MON_I_N15 uses a
10 kOhm/100 nF filter; OC_I_N15 uses a separate 10 kOhm branch without that
telemetry capacitor. Channel B gives MON_V_N15 = -0.1 x N15; -15 V becomes
+1.50 V. Both telemetry time constants are nominally 1 ms. BAT54S clamp
polarities are correct. The four differential gain-network resistors specify
0.1% tolerance. Their resistor-only worst-case common-mode leakage at -15 V
is approximately +/-57 mV, or +/-28.6 mA equivalent; the complete error budget
must also include amplifier, shunt, comparator, reference and layout errors.

Voltage scales and hardware power-good

INPUT uses (75k + 75k) / 10k, giving rail/16. P15 now uses rail/5.7.
N15 uses -rail/10. All other selected voltage channels use rail/2.
The positive current filters have nominal RC = 10 us. The half-scale voltage
filters have RC = 50 us; INPUT is 93.75 us and repaired P15 is 82.46 us.

MON_V_USB, MON_V_ANA6, MON_V_P15 and MON_V_N15 directly feed the four
2900 comparator channels as well as telemetry. At AON = 3.28 V their nominal
physical rail readiness thresholds are USB 4.4626 V, ANA6 5.3770 V,
P15 13.4504 V and |N15| 13.1200 V. These follow AON and upstream ratios;
they are not precision absolute thresholds. There is no local hysteresis.

Shared overcurrent and interlocks

Fourteen INA301 open-drain ALERT outputs and the 3200 negative-current
comparator share OC_OK_N. A fault pulls it LOW and reaches the hardware permit
clear on 2600 independently of telemetry scanning. It also reaches 2400 PA12.
The 3200 reference is AON x 10/57 = 0.57544 V at 3.28 V. With the 3400
2 V/A scale, its nominal trip is 0.28772 A. The comparator reference RC is
82.46 us; this is not a specified complete fault-to-shutdown delay.

R4008 already provides 10 kOhm to AON. Retained R3203 on the reviewed Power
Control sheet adds a parallel 10 kOhm, making the assembled pullup 5 kOhm
and approximately 0.656 mA fault sink current at AON = 3.28 V. These pullups
are compatible; OC_OK_N does not depend on a firmware internal pullup.

3900: Both devices share MUX_A0..A3 and MUX_DISABLE. HIGH disconnects
both COM outputs; LOW enables the chosen current/voltage pair. R4001-R4004
hold address zero and R4007 holds disable HIGH at controller reset. Disabled
COM inputs float, so the buffer/ADC outputs are not valid measurements.

4000: U4001A follows MUX_I_COM; output pin 1 feeds back to IN- pin 2.
U4001B follows MUX_V_COM; output pin 7 feeds back to IN- pin 6. R4005/C4002
and R4006/C4003 use 100 Ohm/1 nF (0.10 us nominal RC). Feedback is taken
before these resistors. ADC_I reaches 2400 PA0/pin 17 and ADC_V PA1/pin 18.
MUX_A0/A1/A2/A3/DISABLE reach PB15/PA8/PA9/PC6/PC7 respectively.

Firmware should disable, update the address, enable and wait for the source,
selector, buffer and ADC acquisition to settle before accepting samples.
The 0.10 us output RC alone does not define this wait. Current and voltage
are a selected pair; the STM32 ADC samples them sequentially. ADC_VTT is a
separate 3300-to-PA2 route; PROG_V_P15 is a separate 2300-to-PA3 route.

Remaining design decisions and verification

- CORE nominal trip 20 A exceeds the TPS568215 8 A converter rating. DDR
  nominal trip 4 A exceeds the TPS62130A 3 A converter rating. The converters'
  own limits may act first. Establish load, inrush and thermal budgets, then
  coordinate external trips with the regulator limits; their resistor values
  were retained rather than selecting an unsupported load budget.
- ANA5 current includes its downstream ANA3V3 load. Coordinate these nested
  thresholds instead of treating them as independent available load budgets.
- Confirm the current input-filter/eFuse leaves, PG_INPUT/FLT_INPUT_N and
  input shunt identity before claiming complete startup-path validation.
- Qualify telemetry and arm logic during AON loss, rail ramp/discharge and
  negative-supply transitions. Check clamp injection and all analog input
  operating limits when adjacent supplies are absent.
- Verify Kelvin shunt routing, gain/offset calibration, comparator noise,
  safe firmware pin defaults, ADC acquisition and actual shutdown on hardware.
