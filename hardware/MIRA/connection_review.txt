MIRA Rev.A — Power Control connection review
2026-10-05

Scope and validation

Reviewed POWER_CONTROL and all ten attached child schematics, 2400 through
3300. The electrical graph was reconstructed from native embedded symbol
pins, wires, junctions, scoped local labels and global labels. KiCad instance
rotation and screen-axis mirroring were included. Manufacturer pinouts and
logic functions were checked against the documents listed below.

Four confirmed connection defects were corrected. A separate final comparison
verified every original pin position, symbol orientation, pin identity and net
partition against the source plus the four explicit repairs. The parent UUID,
all ten child sheet UUIDs, filenames and hierarchy paths were preserved.
The existing MIRA_RevA embedded definitions were retained. R3203 reuses the
existing Panasonic 10 kOhm resistor definition and footprint.

The English guides use the same five headings on every child sheet. The parent
has an individual description of every block and a logical interconnection
overview. Blue annotations distinguish circuit groups. The parent graphics
show signal relationships; the leaf global nets provide the actual electrical
connections. Preview images are approximate renders of native geometry rather
than KiCad plots. All eleven previews were visually inspected and annotations
checked against their drawing frames.

Native KiCad ERC, simulation, firmware validation, PCB checks and bench tests
were not performed. Missing monitor circuits limit whole-system conclusions.

Confirmed defects and completed repairs

1. 2600: U2601 pin 8 (global protection AND output) was unconnected, as was
   U2603 pin 1 (global permit asynchronous clear). Added the local
   GLOBAL_CLEAR_N connection between them. A LOW protection condition now
   reaches the global latch clear instead of leaving that input floating.

2. 2600: U2602 pin 8 (matrix readiness AND output) and U2603 pin 13 (matrix
   permit asynchronous clear) were unconnected. Added MATRIX_CLEAR_N between
   them. These local labels are on one sheet and do not change other nets.

3. 3100: U3103 pin 6 was globally labeled ALL_RAILS_PG. This joined it to
   the push-pull U3018 pin 6 output on 3000 and left the existing
   ALL_COMMANDS_ON input on U2602 pin 4 without its intended driver.
   Renamed the 3100 output to ALL_COMMANDS_ON. Its four local groups were
   renamed CMD_GROUP_0 through CMD_GROUP_3 and the copied hidden logic
   metadata was updated to match the actual CMD inputs.

4. 3200: U3201 pin 2 is open collector, but OC_OK_N had no external pull-up
   in any available schematic. Added R3203 = 10 kOhm from OC_OK_N to AON.
   At AON = 3.28 V its approximate fault sink current is 0.328 mA. The
   comparator output, resistor branch and global label share one continuous
   wire with a visible junction. A firmware-selected internal pull-up is not
   required to establish the released hardware level during MCU reset.

Documentation correction: J2502 now reads HOST MANUAL RESET CONTACT because
it connects to the host supervisor MR input. Its electrical pins did not move.
Several label/field positions were adjusted solely for legibility.

Verified circuit relationships

2400 — Dedicated power sequencer
U2401 is STM32G0B1RBT6, GP LQFP64. Its embedded supply and GPIO pin mapping
matches the GP part, including PD8/PD9 at pins 40/41. USART3 AF0 can use
these UART pins. PA0/PA1/PA2/PA3 receive ADC_I/ADC_V/ADC_VTT/PROG_V_P15.
Permits and externally driven status signals must be firmware inputs; enable,
arm and kill requests must initialize safely. Use internal clocking because
PF0 drives EN_REQ_USB. Keep NRST and SWD enabled and VREFBUF disabled while
VREF+ is tied to AON. FPGA matrix timing and STM32H745 neuron computation
remain outside this power sequencer.

2500 — Independent supervision
U2501 uses AON; U2502 uses SYS3V3. Both have SET1 HIGH and CWD unconnected:
watchdog timeout is 1.60 s nominal (1.36–1.84 s specified). Falling WDI edges
service the timers. Reset release delay and WDO LOW pulse are 200 ms nominal.
D2501 common-anode BAT54A correctly combines AON_RESET_N and PM_WDO_N into
PM_RESET_N. No diode rotation was needed. Host watchdog expiry inhibits the
matrix but does not locally combine WDO into the host RESET output.

2600 — Hardware permission and fault memory
The restored clear chains implement these conditions (AND of every item):

GLOBAL_CLEAR_N:
  AON_RESET_N, PM_WDO_N, PG_INPUT, FLT_INPUT_N, OC_OK_N,
  global stop/kill healthy node, PM_RESET_N.

MATRIX_CLEAR_N:
  GLOBAL_PWR_PERMIT, ALL_RAILS_PG, ALL_COMMANDS_ON, HOST_WDO_N,
  HOST_RESET_N, matrix stop/kill healthy node.

U2603 D inputs are HIGH and presets inactive. A rising arm edge can set a
permit only while its clear is HIGH. Any LOW clear asynchronously removes
that permit. Recovery alone does not re-arm. A global fault also removes the
matrix condition. Stops are CLOSE TO TRIP; an open cable is not detected.
Startup AON reset and clean arm timing still require system verification.

2700 / 2800 — Fifteen qualified enable commands
All eight primary and seven auxiliary channels use:
  CMD_x = EN_REQ_x AND GLOBAL_PWR_PERMIT.
Request and command pull-downs are present. The auxiliary spare gate has
grounded inputs and an NC output. Clearing the global permit suppresses all
commands independently of requests. Converter shutdown and discharge times
belong to their connected rail circuits.

2900 — Four analog readiness channels
Outputs PG_USB, PG_ANA, PG_P15 and PG_N15 have 10 kOhm AON pull-ups.
For AON = 3.28 V, the reference thresholds at the MON inputs are:
  MON_V_USB:   2.2313 V
  MON_V_ANA6:  2.6885 V
  MON_V_P15:   2.3597 V
  MON_V_N15:   1.3120 V
C2901–C2904 filter REFERENCES, with nominal time constants approximately
32, 18, 28 and 60 us. They do not filter the MON inputs or specify a fixed
power-good qualification delay. No analog feedback hysteresis is drawn.
Actual rail thresholds require the missing monitor scaling and polarity.

3000 / 3100 — Separate readiness and command aggregates
3000 ANDs fifteen measured PG/PGR signals into ALL_RAILS_PG.
3100 ANDs fifteen qualified CMD signals into ALL_COMMANDS_ON.
Each corrected global has one explicit push-pull driver. The local group
labels on the two sheets are independent. Global startup permission does
not depend on switched-rail readiness, allowing startup without a logical
deadlock. Matrix permission requires both aggregates; intentionally disabling
any included rail prevents matrix authorization. Neither tree contains a
timer or latch.

3200 — Negative-current check
Reference = AON x 10 / 57 = 0.57544 V at AON = 3.28 V.
C3201 filters this reference; (47 kOhm || 10 kOhm) x 10 nF = 82.46 us.
OC_I_N15 above the reference pulls OC_OK_N LOW and clears the global permit.
The conditioned current-sense producer is missing from the supplied scope,
so a trip current in amperes cannot be calculated or validated.

3300 — Debug and host interface
J3301: 1 AON reference, 2 GND, 3 SWDIO, 4 SWCLK, 5 PM_RESET_N.
J3302: 1 SYS3V3 reference, 2/9/10 GND, 3 buffered PM UART TX,
4 HOST_UART_TX, 5 HOST_WDI, 6 buffered MATRIX_PERMIT,
7 buffered ALL_RAILS_PG, 8 buffered GLOBAL_PWR_PERMIT.
HOST_RESET_N is not on J3302. U3301 supplies the four host-facing outputs
from SYS3V3 and supports partial power down. Host receive is pulled LOW
when undriven; firmware must handle this UART break/off condition.
R3304/C3301 form the nominal 10 us VTT ADC filter, with BAT54S rail clamps.

Remaining integration checks

- No producer for OC_I_N15 or MON_V_USB/ANA6/P15/N15 exists in the available
  native schematics. Do not attach raw negative voltage to the AON inputs.
  Restore or supply the documented conditioning circuits before relying on
  these protections. Current gain/offset and real rail thresholds are unknown.
- ADC_I/ADC_V and the telemetry multiplexer continue to unprovided circuitry.
  The available file named 1200_POWER_MONITOR contains a CORE regulator,
  not the required monitor/multiplexer. Do not infer its function from its name.
- SYS3V3 does have a producer on the earlier 700_SYS_3V3 sheet. The host
  supervisor output below its POR level is undefined; AON pull-ups can make
  off-rail status look HIGH. Independent measured readiness must inhibit arm.
- Confirm the external host supplies HOST_WDI and receives reset through a
  separately defined path. Verify firmware recovery policy for host expiry.
- Verify all fifteen rails are intentionally required for matrix operations,
  ADC input settling and clamp injection, reset LOW margins, AON startup,
  comparator noise, arm/clear timing and actual shutdown on hardware.
